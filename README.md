## Civic AI Club

A student dev lab at Pomfret School (40+ members) that builds tools for school departments and local organizations, like a planner that pulls study hall students' Canvas assignments into Google Docs, and a tool that sets up report comment documents for teachers. I'm its Founder and President.

[Organization](https://github.com/CivicAIClub) · [Website](https://civicaiclub.github.io/)

## OurCityHealth

A free civic dashboard showing how cities are doing on things like housing, safety and cost of living, and what's driving the changes.

[Live demo](https://caydenauyang.github.io/OurCityHealth/) · [Code](https://github.com/CaydenAuyang/OurCityHealth)

## Screen-parsing GUI agent

A macOS agent that reads the screen with computer vision and OCR and uses a language model to carry out tasks across apps and websites. I built it during my summer 2025 internship at Wisers Information in Hong Kong, on top of Microsoft's OmniParser.

[Code](https://github.com/CaydenAuyang/screen-parsing-gui-agent)

## Smaller projects

- [Inferno Acoustic Map](https://caydenauyang.github.io/Inferno-Acoustic-Map/): an interactive map of how sound changes as Dante descends through Hell, from the noise of the Styx to the silence of Cocytus.
- [Han Dynasty Jeopardy](https://caydenauyang.github.io/Han-Dynasty-Jeopardy/): a Jeopardy-style review game about the Han Dynasty, with a video explanation for each clue.
- [STORM](https://github.com/CaydenAuyang/STORM): an audiovisual storm installation in Three.js that reacts to the sound and movement in the room through the microphone and webcam.
