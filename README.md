## Civic Exchange, summer 2026

Intern at Civic Exchange, a public policy think tank in Hong Kong. Its 25 years of research sat in folders nobody could search. I started with 11 staff interviews, then built 12 searchable notebooks covering about 2,350 documents, where every answer cites its source, plus writing and translation assistants grounded in its own reports. I handed it all over with a 39-page manual and a staff workshop.

## Civic AI Club

A student dev lab at Pomfret School (40+ members). School offices and local organizations bring us a problem, and a student team builds the tool and hands it over. We have eight cases this year. In October we delivered two: AutoPlanner, which pulls each study hall student's Canvas assignments into a planner Doc for the CLC Supported Study Hall, and Music Studio, a lesson and booking portal for the Music Department. I'm its Founder and President.

[Organization](https://github.com/CivicAIClub) · [Website](https://civicaiclub.github.io/)

## OurCityHealth

A free dashboard that scores cities from what local news reports. So far it scores seven: Hong Kong, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Singapore, Tokyo, London and New York. This fall I'm running an independent study, Measuring What Matters, that tests whether the news actually tracks how Hong Kong, New York and London are doing.

[Live demo](https://caydenauyang.github.io/OurCityHealth/) · [Code](https://github.com/CaydenAuyang/OurCityHealth)

## Screen-parsing GUI agent

A macOS agent that reads the screen with computer vision and OCR and uses a language model to carry out tasks across apps and websites. I built it during my summer 2025 internship at Wisers Information in Hong Kong, on top of Microsoft's OmniParser.

[Code](https://github.com/CaydenAuyang/screen-parsing-gui-agent)
